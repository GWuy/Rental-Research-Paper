\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}
This working paper defines a candidate event-driven microservices architecture for rental property management and a protocol for evaluating it. The seven service names supplied in the project brief provide a starting point for separating business responsibility and data ownership. The design identifies where synchronous calls may be needed, where domain events may decouple workflows, and why outbox publication and idempotent consumption must be examined together. The proposed evaluation covers request latency and throughput, event processing, duplicate handling, and recovery after injected failures. No quantitative result or implemented guarantee is claimed at this stage.

The next step is to reconcile every service, interface, role, event, and deployment component with the system specification and source code. The team should then complete the literature review, implement or verify the proposed consistency mechanisms, execute the experiments with retained raw data, and revise the discussion according to observed behavior.
