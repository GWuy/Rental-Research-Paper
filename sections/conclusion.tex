\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper describes an event-driven design for rental property management. The specified acceptance workflow links the transaction in Listing and Booking to draft creation in Contract and Tenant through a Kafka event. The evaluation protocol follows that link from the gateway response through the outbox, broker, and committed business effect. Its central consistency criterion is that each accepted request produces one draft contract after processing settles, including when the same event is delivered again.

The current contribution is a design and a protocol for evaluating it. Empirical findings on throughput, latency, and recovery require runs of a versioned implementation with retained evidence. Follow-up experiments should measure the API and the asynchronous workflow under sustained load and exercise the publisher and consumer failures at the defined points. Additional workloads can examine whether the observed behavior extends to contract signing and billing, where several services act on the same contract event.
